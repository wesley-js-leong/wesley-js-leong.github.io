Hi! I'm Wesley Leong (he/him).

I am a sixth-year PhD candidate in Psychology (*on the academic job market!*) at the University of Connecticut, where I work primarily with Gerry Altmann and Eiling Yee. I use behavioral experiments and EEG to study how our minds and brains represent things in working memory and language. Here are some current questions:

1. What neural processes underlie how we represent objects that change? Do we recruit the same mechanisms that allow us to represent distinct objects in a sequence?

2. How do we bind different states of an object, at different points in time, into a single coherent object representation?

3. How does similarity structure our knowledge of labels and concepts? Do people prefer similar labels referring to similar things, and if so, why?
